About

I’m a designer, web developer, and illustrator from Barcelona. I've been turning ideas into images since 2003. Over the years, my work has focused mostly on Education, Peacebuilding, and Culture. I've worked across a wide range of areas, including branding, web production, signage design or book illustration.

I like helping teams to develop their ideas, and I can also manage projects from scratch, contributing knowledge from other areas such as graphic design, illustration or animation. I can guide you on what type of service is most suitable and if I am the right person to develop it. I love most of all, helping others tell stories.
